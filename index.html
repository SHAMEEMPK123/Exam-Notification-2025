NID DAT 2026 – Design Aptitude Test

The entrance examination for the National Institute of Design’s B.Des and M.Des programmes — conducted by NID via the DAT (Design Aptitude Test) for admission into their multiple campuses.

Key Details

  • Registration Start Date: 11 September 2025
  • Last Date for Submission of Application Form: 1 December 2025 (by 11:59 pm)
  • Application Edit/Correction Window: 2–4 December 2025
  • Issue of Admit Card for Prelims: 11 December 2025
  • Date of DAT Prelims (B.Des & M.Des): Sunday, 21 December 2025
  • Result of Prelims – B.Des: 7 April 2026
  • Date of DAT Mains & further stages: Tentatively April/May 2026

Eligibility

For B.Des: Candidates who have completed or are appearing in 10+2 (any stream) from a recognised board. For M.Des: Candidates with a bachelor’s degree in any discipline.

Programme Offered

B.Des (Honours) – Undergraduate Design Programme

Four‐year design programmes offered at multiple NID campuses including Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh and Assam.

M.Des – Postgraduate Design Programme

Master of Design programmes at NID (various campuses) with specialisations and studio-based evaluation.

For full syllabus, exam pattern, fee structure, campus-wise intake and updates, refer to the official admissions handbook and website: NID DAT 2026 Official website.

UPES Entrance Test 2026 – UPES Admission Examinations

UPES conducts its own admission tests (such as UPESEAT, UPESDAT, UPESMET-UG, etc.) for various undergraduate and postgraduate programs at UPES, Dehradun.

Key Details

  • Last Date to Apply: TBA – as listed on the official “Important Dates” page.
  • Minimum Eligibility (UG / B.Tech): Minimum 50% marks in Class X & XII; for B.Tech (PCM) a stream with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics.
  • Application Process: Online registration, form-fill and payment (fee approx. ₹1,750 for many UG programmes).
  • Exam Date(s): Varies by programme – e.g., for UPESEAT 2025: Phase I May 2–4; Phase II May 31–June 1; Phase III June 25–26.

Selection & Programs Covered

Undergraduate Engineering & Technology (B.Tech / B.E.)

Admission through UPESEAT or accepted national tests such as JEE Main/CUET; across specialisations like Computer Science, Electrical, Mechanical, etc.

Design / Management / Other UG & PG Programmes

For example: B.Des/M.Des via UPESDAT, BBA via UPESMET-UG, MBA via UPESMET etc.

For detailed eligibility, syllabus, test pattern, and latest updates, visit the official admissions page of UPES: visit the official admissions page of UPES.

BITSDAT 2026 – BITS Design Aptitude Test

The BITSDAT (Bachelor of Design Admissions) is conducted by Birla Institute of Technology & Science, Pilani – Mumbai Campus (BITS Pilani – Mumbai) via its Design School for the four-year B.Des (Honours) programme offered by BITS Design School.

Key Details

  • Registration Start Date: 16 October 2025
  • Last Date to Apply: 1 February 2026
  • BITSDAT Exam Dates: 31 March to 5 April 2026
  • Shortlist / Result Declaration: 16 April 2026
  • Portfolio Upload & Evaluation: 16-20 April 2026
  • Online Interview: 2-12 May 2026

Eligibility

Candidates must have passed (or be appearing in) Class 12 (or equivalent) with the required minimum marks (≈ 45% or equivalent). Admission is based on valid scores in one of the accepted entrance exams: NID DAT Prelims / UCEED / BITSDAT.

Programme Offered

B.Des (Hons) – Four-Year Programme

Residential trans-disciplinary design honours programme covering specialisations such as Physical Product & Digital Product Design (UI/UX), Design Research & Insights, Experience Design, Visual Design and more.

For detailed syllabus, fee structure, selection process, and latest updates, visit the official admissions page of BITS Design School: bitsdesign.edu.in/admissions.

JEE Main 2026 – Joint Entrance Examination (Main)

The Joint Entrance Examination (Main), conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), is the national-level test for admission into B.E./B.Tech, B.Arch, and B.Planning programs at NITs, IIITs, and other centrally funded technical institutions, as well as eligibility for JEE (Advanced) for admission to IITs.

Key Details

  • Online Submission of Application Form: 31 October 2025 to 27 November 2025 (up to 09:00 P.M.)
  • Last Date for Fee Payment (Credit/Debit Card/Net-Banking/UPI): 27 November 2025 (up to 11:50 P.M.)
  • Announcement of City of Examination: By the First week of January 2026
  • Downloading of Admit Cards: Will be announced later on the website
  • Date(s) of Examination: Between 21 January to 30 January 2026
  • Centre, Date, and Shift: As indicated in the Admit Card
  • Display of Recorded Responses and Answer Keys: To be announced later on the website
  • Declaration of Result: By 12 February 2026 (Tentative)
  • Official Websites: https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/, https://www.nta.ac.in/

Eligibility

Candidates should have passed or be appearing in Class 12 (or equivalent) examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, or Technical Vocational subject.

Courses Offered

B.E. / B.Tech Programs

Offered by NITs, IIITs, and participating institutions in diverse branches such as:
• Computer Science and Engineering
• Electronics and Communication Engineering
• Mechanical Engineering
• Civil Engineering
• Electrical and Electronics Engineering
• Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

B.Arch and B.Planning Programs

For students aspiring for careers in Architecture and Urban Planning:
• B.Arch (Paper 2A)
• B.Planning (Paper 2B)

For detailed syllabus, pattern, and updates, visit the official JEE Main 2026 website.

VITEEE 2026 – VIT Engineering Entrance Examination

VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination) is conducted by Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) for admission to its prestigious B.Tech programs across Vellore, Chennai, Bhopal, and Amaravati campuses. The exam serves as the gateway for students from both Mathematics and Biology streams.

Key Details

  • Exam Date: April 27 – May 03, 2026
  • Application End Date: March 31, 2026
  • Eligibility: Candidates should have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.
  • Application Process: Online registration and submission through the official VITEEE portal.

Courses Offered

B.Tech Programs (Engineering Stream)

Core and interdisciplinary branches available across all campuses, including:
• Computer Science and Engineering
• Electronics and Communication Engineering
• Mechanical Engineering
• Civil Engineering
• Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
• Electrical and Electronics Engineering

B.Tech Programs for Biology Students

Biology stream students (PCB) are eligible for the following specialized programs:
• Biotechnology
• Biomedical Engineering
• Bioinformatics
• Food Technology
• Genetic Engineering

For detailed eligibility, syllabus, and application guidelines, visit the official VITEEE 2026 website.
