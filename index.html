IIM Bangalore – New Undergraduate Programs

IIM Bangalore’s new Undergraduate Programs (BSc Honours - Economics and BSc Honours - Data Science) will start admissions in the third week of October 2025, with classes beginning in August 2026.

Key Details

  • End Date (Admissions): Application closes late October 2025.
  • Eligibility: 12th grade pass (any stream), strong academic performance.
  • Application Process: Online form available on the official IIMB UG admission portal.

Courses Offered

BSc Honours in Economics

With a Data Science minor.

BSc Honours in Data Science

With an Economics minor.

CLAT 2025 Exam Notification

  • Exam Date: December 1, 2025 (Sunday), 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM (with extension for PwD candidates)
  • Applications Open: July 15, 2024
  • Applications Close: October 15, 2024 (with possible extension up to October 22, 2024)
  • Eligibility:
    UG: Passed 10+2 with minimum 45% marks (40% for SC/ST/PwD). Students appearing in 2025 can also apply.
    PG: LLB degree with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD)
  • Application Process: Online application available on the official website of the Consortium of NLUs
  • Exam Mode: Offline (pen and paper)
  • Exam Sections: Legal aptitude, current affairs, general knowledge, English, math, logical reasoning
  • This exam is for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate law programs at National Law Universities across India
