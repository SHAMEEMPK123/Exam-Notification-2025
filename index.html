VITEEE 2026 – VIT Engineering Entrance Examination

VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination) is conducted by Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) for admission to its prestigious B.Tech programs across Vellore, Chennai, Bhopal, and Amaravati campuses. The exam serves as the gateway for students from both Mathematics and Biology streams.

Key Details

  • Exam Date: April 27 – May 03, 2026
  • Application End Date: March 31, 2026
  • Eligibility: Candidates should have passed or be appearing in Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology.
  • Application Process: Online registration and submission through the official VITEEE portal.

Courses Offered

B.Tech Programs (Engineering Stream)

Core and interdisciplinary branches available across all campuses, including:
• Computer Science and Engineering
• Electronics and Communication Engineering
• Mechanical Engineering
• Civil Engineering
• Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
• Electrical and Electronics Engineering

B.Tech Programs for Biology Students

Biology stream students (PCB) are eligible for the following specialized programs:
• Biotechnology
• Biomedical Engineering
• Bioinformatics
• Food Technology
• Genetic Engineering

For detailed eligibility, syllabus, and application guidelines, visit the official VITEEE 2026 website.

CLAT 2025 Exam Notification

  • Exam Date: December 7, 2025 (Sunday)
  • Applications Open: August 01 , 2025
  • Applications Close: October 31, 2025
  • Eligibility:
    UG: Passed 10+2 with minimum 45% marks (40% for SC/ST/PwD). Students appearing in 2025 can also apply.
    PG: LLB degree with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/PwD)
  • Application Process: Online application available on the official website of the Consortium of NLUs
  • Exam Mode: Offline (pen and paper)
  • Exam Sections: Legal aptitude, current affairs, general knowledge, English, math, logical reasoning
  • This exam is for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate law programs at National Law Universities across India
