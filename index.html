CUET (UG) 2026 – Common University Entrance Test

The Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) 2026 is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission into Undergraduate programmes of Central Universities (CUs) and other participating Universities (State / Deemed / Private) across India and abroad. The examination will be conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

Key Details

  • Application Start Date: January 03, 2026
  • Application End Date: January 30, 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
  • Last Date for Fee Payment: January 31, 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
  • Application Correction Window: February 02 – February 04, 2026
  • Exam Date: Between May 11 and May 31, 2026 (Tentative)
  • Mode of Exam: Computer Based Test (CBT)

Eligibility

Undergraduate Admissions

Candidates who have passed or are appearing in Class 12 or equivalent examination are eligible. Eligibility criteria may vary depending on the university and programme selected.

Participating Universities

Central & Participating Universities

Admissions through CUET (UG) 2026 will be offered by Central Universities and other participating State, Deemed, and Private Universities across the country.

For detailed updates and official notifications, visit the official CUET (UG) 2026 website.

Amrita AEEE 2026 – Amrita Entrance Examination – Engineering (AEEE)

AEEE is the national-level computer-based test for B.Tech admissions to Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (multiple campuses). Admissions are also offered on the basis of JEE Main percentile for candidates who choose that route.

Key Details

  • Last Date to Apply: January 15, 2026
  • Slot Booking Starts: January 10, 2026
  • Exam Dates (AEEE Phase I): January 30 – February 01, 2026
  • Mode & Duration: Computer Based Test (CBT), 150 minutes (2.5 hours)
  • Exam Cities / Centres: More than 100 cities across India — slot & city as per booking.
  • Selection Routes: AEEE rank OR JEE Main percentile — candidates register for the Centralised Seat Allotment Process (CSAP) after results.
  • Official Apply / Handbook: spotlight.amrita.edu/btech

Important Dates (Quick)

  • Application Deadline: 15 January 2026
  • Slot Booking: From 10 January 2026
  • AEEE Exam Window: 30 Jan – 01 Feb 2026
  • Centralised Seat Allotment: May 2026 (CSAP) — registration after AEEE/JEE results.

Eligibility

Candidates should have passed 10+2 (or equivalent) with the required aggregate and subjectwise minimums. Typical requirement (example): minimum 60% aggregate in PCM and at least 50% in Mathematics — consult the official handbook for campus/programme-specific criteria.

Exam Pattern

Total duration: 150 minutes. Sectional composition (AEEE CBT): Mathematics — 40 questions; Physics — 30 questions; Chemistry — 20 questions; Quantitative Aptitude — 10 questions; English — 10 questions. Total = 110 MCQs.

Scholarships & Seats

B.Tech scholarships are awarded to top candidates registering through CSAP using AEEE ranks or JEE Main percentile. A significant portion of seats are earmarked under scholarship categories — check the official brochure for exact scholarship rules and seat breakup.

How to Apply

  1. Click Apply Now on the Amrita B.Tech admissions page and create your account (or use the AEEE portal).
  2. Complete the online application form, upload required documents and pay the registration fee (refer to the handbook for the exact fee).
  3. Book your preferred exam slot when slot booking opens, download admit card, and appear for the CBT.
  4. After results, register for CSAP (if seeking admission via AEEE/JEE route) and follow counselling instructions.

Contact & Support

For application queries: aeee@amrita.edu | Phone: 08069150838. Campus B.Tech contacts and addresses are listed on the official admissions page.

For the full handbook, slot booking links, FAQs, and campus-wise details, visit the official Amrita B.Tech admissions page: spotlight.amrita.edu/btech.
